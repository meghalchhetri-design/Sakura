                ABOUT SAKURA

                Women's health deserves more understanding.

                Sakura is designed as an educational companion for menstrual, hormonal and reproductive health. It helps organize information around conditions, nutrition and supportive care in a way that feels easier to explore.

                It does not diagnose conditions or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
